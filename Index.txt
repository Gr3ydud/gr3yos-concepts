Gr3yOS
======

Minimal, modular Linux-based operating system.
Designed to be tiny, stable, and user-hosted.

---------

    curl -O https://Gr3ydud.github.io/gr3yos-site/links.txt STATUS: unreleased

[3] Optional Testing Tools
    curl -O https://Gr3ydud.github.io/gr3yos-site/links.txt STATUS: WORKING ON IT

Notes
-----
- Minimal, CLI-first OS
- No automatic updates
- Modular
- Experimental builds may have bugs
- Recommended for users familiar with Linux
- i am one person, do not expect a perfectly kept OS.

CLI Users
---------
Use 'curl' or 'wget' to download files.
No browser required, though a browser is reccommended.
